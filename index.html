    How these were made

    • Every builder read the guide's original source (Google Doc, Notion page, prompt page or PDF), not just the library summary. Several summaries were shorter than the originals, so the full versions were used.
    • All brands, products, prices and people are fictional unless a source is cited on the site. Facts (NASA planet data, the 1953 North Sea flood, Jules Verne quotations) link to their sources.
    • Photos and third-party components carry their licences and authors on the site that uses them.
    • Every site was checked on desktop and phone sizes, for reduced motion, and with the Impeccable design detector.
