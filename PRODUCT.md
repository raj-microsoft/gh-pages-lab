# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: one static HTML page, self-hosted fonts, WebP thumbnails. GitHub Pages.

## Users

Raj Balakrishnan and anyone he shares it with: people curious how far free tools and public AI design guides can take a website.

## Product Purpose

An index of 12 sites, each built by following one guide from Raj's AI Web Design Library. Success: a visitor sees all 12 at a glance, opens any one, and understands which guide it followed and what was swapped for a free tool.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Each entry: live link, repo link, the guide it follows (linked to the original source), what was substituted.
- Honest: all brands, prices and people in the projects are fictional unless cited.

## Product Principles

- The work leads; the index stays quiet.
- Every entry traceable to its guide.
